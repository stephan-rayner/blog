## Speaker Bio

### Short (one to two sentences)

Stephan Rayner is a senior engineer at Grafana Labs who thinks engineering is as much an interpersonal discipline as a technical one. He blogs about the technical and human sides of the job, from column-oriented databases to what makes a piece of documentation actually hold up under pressure.

### Standard (submitted version)

Stephan Rayner is a senior engineer at Grafana Labs. He has held positions as both an IC and a manager. Much of his career has been in data engineering and machine learning.

Stephan writes a blog where he argues that engineering is fundamentally an interpersonal discipline, and that knowledge sharing, not raw technical skill, is what separates a good engineer from a great one.

In his personal life, Stephan enjoys cooking, dancing, hiking, and mushroom foraging.
